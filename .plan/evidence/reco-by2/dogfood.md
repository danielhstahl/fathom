## What this does and does not establish

**It establishes** that `fathom check` completes on three repositories of different shape and language,
cloned whole and untouched by this project, and returns an answer a reviewer could act on: `api/breaking`
with a file and line and the item that went away, `dependencies/major-version` naming the lockfile hunk,
`tests` measuring episodes by added lines per surface. It establishes that the checks that genuinely cannot
judge say so: `layering` is `blocked` on all three because a corpus repository has no `fathom.toml`, and it
prints the remedy; on `ruff`, `api` is `blocked` at its 6 000-files-per-side ceiling rather than reporting
"no breaking changes" about a surface it did not read. The dogfood job therefore `--require`s the three
checks that always have something to say over a corpus, and prints the blocked rows instead of hiding them.

**It does not establish** precision or recall on third-party code. Nothing here is labelled, and 37 findings
is a count, not a truth; Guardrail 3 (`tools/calibrate_g3.py`) owns that question and its bars are unchanged
by this run. What a corpus run buys is the failure modes calibration cannot reach: histories two orders of
magnitude longer than the fixture's, languages the extractors were not written for, ceilings set for a
medium repository, and a clone nobody tuned.

**One earlier reading of this table was wrong, and the correction is the useful part.** An earlier pass
computed the base with `git rev-list --skip=200 --max-parents=0 HEAD`, which does not mean "200 commits
back": `--max-parents=0` restricts the walk to root commits, so the expression returned nothing, the
fallback handed back the root commit, and a "200-commit window" silently became the entire history of the
repository — which is why the first `cli` row claimed 254 findings over a window it had not checked. The
workflow now uses `git rev-list --skip=200 -n 1 HEAD` with the root commit only as the fallback, and the
range it actually checked is printed in every row, from the report rather than from the shell's arithmetic.
